As far as the spatial distribution of values go, the lowest values are found in the areas with the highest population density, the Chicago metro area and into the surrounding suburbs. The outliers to this trend can be found in Chanpaign and Coles counties, which have college towns and obesity rates lower than surrounding counties. The rural areas have higher values on average, but the region with the highest values are in south central Illinois. 
The Chicago metro having a cluster of low values makes sense because obesity, in general, decreases in more urbanized areas. The highest concentration of counties with the highest rates of obesity are found along Illinois's borders with Indiana and Wisconsin.
