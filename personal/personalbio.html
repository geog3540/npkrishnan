    About

    My research interests lie in studying the economic, social, and environmental effects that water pollution causes. I am interested in understanding how natural systems and human systems are intertwined and how this relationship affects aspects of human life. As an undergraduate student at the University of Iowa, I have taken courses such as Geographic Visualization, GIS for Environmental Applications, and Intro to Geographic Databases. These courses have allowed me to become proficient in GIS technology, data collection, data analysis. Outside of university courses, I have undertaken research to explore this relationship within the Iowa River watershed. During this research, I explored how the river network, in conjunction with human activity that creates pollution, has lead to Iowa's high cancer rate. From a professional standpoint, I hope to find employment in water management, which would allow me to further explore how humans and nature work together to impact people.
