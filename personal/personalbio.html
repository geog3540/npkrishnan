    About

    My research interests lie in studying the economic, social, and environmental effects that water pollution causes. I am interested in understanding how natural systems and human systems are intertwined and how this relationship affects aspects of human life. I have undertaken research to explore this relationship within the Iowa River watershed. During this research, I explored how the river network, in conjunction with human activity that creates pollution, has lead to Iowa's high cancer rate. From a professional standpoint, I hope to find employment in water management, which would allow me to further explore how humans and nature work together to impact people.
